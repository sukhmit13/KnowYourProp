The calculator's mode depends on the user's relationship to the property
Investor · buy-to-leaseNOI → DSCR / cap rate (above). Residential and/or commercial rent is income. Standard income-property underwriting.
Owner-occupant · SBAGlobal / business cash-flow DSCR. If they occupy the commercial space, that's saved rent (imputed), not income; residential rent offsets occupancy cost. SBA 504/7(a), ≥51% owner-occupancy.
Tenant · leasing spaceNot buying the building. Commercial rent is an expense; their revenue is a business projection → a rent-affordability check, not an NOI calc.
