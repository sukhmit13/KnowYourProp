## The hierarchy rule to preserve
- **Step 2** (dark navy, big shadow) = heaviest, the headline.
- **Steps 1 & 3** (white cards, small shadow) = defined panels that hold their own next to the dark band.
- **Step 4** (paper, no shadow) = deliberately quiet reference.

Do not give Step 4 a shadow and do not darken Steps 1/3 — the contrast between the three weights is what makes the flow readable.

## Acceptance check
Reading top to bottom, the eye should follow 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 via the numbered indigo chips, and the light input/coverage cards should no longer feel like they vanish next to the dark band. Nothing black — all chips/numbers/accents use indigo, green, or gold per the tokens.
