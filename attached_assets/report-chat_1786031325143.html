Outside source
TIF (Tax Increment Financing) is a tool Chicago uses to fund development in areas that need investment. When a district is created, its current property-tax revenue becomes a baseline; as values rise, the extra revenue (the "increment") goes into a fund spent on improvements inside that district, typically for 23 years.
