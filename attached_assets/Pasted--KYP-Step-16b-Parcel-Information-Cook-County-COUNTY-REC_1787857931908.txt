The body has **three mutually exclusive data branches**, plus an overlay:

| Branch | Fires when | Fields |
|---|---|---|

## 1. The finding that matters most — the commercial figures are a model, not a record

On a Class 5 parcel the section prints **NOI, Cap Rate, Effective Gross Income, Potential Gross
Income, Vacancy Rate, Expense Ratio, Rent/SF** and an **Investment Rating**, in the same grid, at the
same weight, as Building Size and Year Built.

**They are not the same kind of fact.** Building size is observed. Those figures are the assessor's
**inputs to a valuation model** — the vacancy and expense ratios are class-wide assumptions, and the
cap rate is the one the assessor applied, not one any transaction produced. A reader who takes a 7%
cap rate off this screen as market has been misled by the layout, not by the data.

**Required treatment:**

1. Move them into their own panel under a subhead, **`HOW THE ASSESSOR VALUED IT`**.
2. `.kyp-tiles.modelled` — the same filled tiles, wrapped in a dashed indigo-wash frame. Same shape as the record figures, visibly marked as a different kind of claim.
3. A `.kyp-modhd` header immediately above, stating in plain words that these are modelled and
   **must not be underwritten from**. Not a footnote.
4. Rename the labels to carry the epistemics: `Assumed NOI`, `Applied cap rate`, `Assumed vacancy`.
   A label that says `NOI` claims the building earned it.

### 1.1 Cut `Investment Rating`

It is a letter grade the county derives from the model inputs directly above it. Reprinting it turns
a valuation artefact into a property quality score, and it is the single most likely field on this
screen to be quoted out of context. **Everything it derives from stays** — state that in a
`.kyp-footnote` so the cut is visible rather than silent.

## 2. Structure

Two numbered subheads. This section has genuinely separate panels, so unlike Historic Status it earns
them.

1. Three hero blocks
2. `.kyp-caveat` — co-parcel / lookup-state warnings, **above the blocks** (§4)
3. `08.1 WHAT THE COUNTY RECORDS` — `.kyp-unitwrap`, then `.kyp-tiles`, then `.kyp-recgrid`, then `.kyp-yn`
4. `.kyp-src` — PIN provenance, refresh, assessor and treasurer links
5. `08.2 HOW THE ASSESSOR VALUED IT` — Class 5 only
6. `.kyp-footnote`, manual-entry caveat, `.kyp-src`

Numbers derive from `accProps.index` and renumber sequentially over the subheads that render — on a
residential parcel `08.2` does not exist, so `08.1` is the only one and **no number renders at all**
(one subhead is not a division).

### 2.1 Hero blocks

| # | `.bv` | `.bl` | `.bbreak` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | apartments | `Apartments on the county record` | `Class` n + description · `Assessed at` 10% of market value |
| 2 | building sf | `Building sf on a {land} sf lot` | `Coverage` % — derived · `Per unit` sf — derived |
| 3 | year built | `Built — {n} years old` | `Condition` · `As of` the {yr} assessment |

All indigo. A classification is a state, not a verdict. Coverage and per-unit are **derived, not
recorded** — say so in the row, as above.

On a commercial parcel block 1 becomes `Total units` and block 2 uses `commercialData.bldgSf` /
`landSf`. Where apartments is absent, drop block 1 and use `kyp-blocks hero two`.

### 2.2 Use the components the rest of the report already uses

The report has a settled vocabulary for this. Match it rather than inventing a fourth grid:

| Established pattern | Where it is used today | Use here for |
|---|---|---|
| Large filled blocks, indigo / dark / verdict | Permits' `12 · $248K · 1 NOT CLOSED OUT` row | the three hero blocks |
| Small filled tiles, one indigo + the rest dark | the zoning card's `MAX FAR / MAX HEIGHT / PARKING / MIN LOT AREA` | the four figures that matter — `.kyp-tiles` |
| Quiet label/value grid, hairline separated | the permit card's `PERMIT NO. / OWNER OF RECORD` block | every plain recorded field — `.kyp-recgrid` |
| ✓ / ✗ marked rows | the zoning card's `✓ Residential / ✗ Industrial` | recorded booleans — `.kyp-yn` |

**An earlier draft gave every field its own white outlined mini card.** That was a fifth pattern the
report does not use, and 36 identical boxes flatten the hierarchy — land size and porch type read as
equally important. Deleted.

**`.kyp-tiles` — four figures, filled.** Land size (indigo), building size, apartments, year built.
The derived values ride in `.s`: `52% lot coverage — derived`, `819 sf per unit — derived`. **Never
more than four.** The moment a fifth tile is tempting, it belongs in the record grid.

**`.kyp-recgrid` — everything else.** 16 fields on a residential parcel, four across, one hairline
between them, no box per field. A record should read as a record, not as sixteen cards. A qualifier
shares its field's cell in `.s` — `Basement / Unfinished`, `Garage / Detached`.

**`.kyp-yn` — recorded booleans.** `Mixed use`, `Condominium`, and whether a building record exists at
all. A mark and a word, never a colour: these are states, not verdicts.

**Absence renders.** A field the county does not carry gets `.kyp-rec.none` — muted, reading
`Not recorded`, with a one-line reason (`condominium field`). A field that simply vanishes is
indistinguishable from one we forgot, and this section has a history of exactly that. Gate it on
fields *expected* for the parcel's class — no 24 empty commercial cells on a two-flat.

**Unit configuration is not part of either grid.** It sits above them in `.kyp-unitwrap` — see §3.

The residential and characteristics branches merge into the one record grid. Where both carry a
field, the characteristics value wins — the current `showBuildingSf`/`showLandSf`/`showYearBuilt`
diffing exists only to avoid printing a field twice, and merging removes the need for it.

## 3. Unit configuration — draw the building, and draw the alternative

Today: `matching = apartments === bedrooms && bedrooms === baths` → either "configured as two matching
1BR / 1BA apartments" or, for everything else, "mixed unit sizes."

**The assessor records building totals, never a unit-by-unit split.** So the honest content is not a
sentence — it is **the set of splits consistent with those totals**. Render it as tiles, one per unit,
in `.kyp-unitwrap`:

- **Solid tiles** = recorded.
- **Dashed tiles** = consistent with the totals but not recorded.
- **A second, muted row** = the alternative distribution that produces identical totals.

That last row is the whole point. "The split is not recorded" is a sentence a reader skims; *two
layouts that both add to 4 beds and 2 baths* is a fact they cannot skim.

| Condition | Tag | Tiles |
|---|---|---|
| `units === beds === baths` | `Determined` (green) | solid, `1 bed / 1 bath` each. **No other distribution is possible** — the only case where a per-unit figure is a fact. |
| `beds % units === 0` | `Split not recorded` (slate) | dashed, the even split, each marked *if divided evenly* — **plus the alternative row**, `{b+1}` and `{b−1}` |
| `beds % units !== 0` | `Units differ` (indigo) | dashed, `Larger unit` / `Smaller unit` with the most likely split. **Non-uniformity is proven** whatever the exact division. No alternative row — every alternative is also uneven, which is the finding. |
| `beds < units` | `Studios likely` (indigo) | one tile per unit; the surplus render `.u.empty` with `—` and *no bedroom recorded* |

The header carries the county totals in full — `county totals · 4 beds · 2 baths · 2 units` — so the
tiles never have to be trusted as the source.

**Two hard rules.** Never print a fractional bedroom — "2.5 beds per unit" describes no apartment
that exists. Never render at all when `apartments` is missing; every statement is a claim about a
division, and with no divisor there is nothing to say.

`halfBaths` prints as `{n} full + {m} half` **in the totals line** and **only the full baths divide** —
a half bath cannot be assigned to a unit by arithmetic.

### 3.1 On a commercial parcel the mix is recorded, so stop inferring

`studioUnits` / `oneBrUnits` / `twoBrUnits` / `threeBrUnits` / `fourBrUnits` is the county's own
breakdown. **Where it exists, render the same component with solid tiles, one per bedroom type, and
none of the four states above run.** Label it `Unit mix`, tag it `Recorded`, and show each type's
share of the total. It is a record, not a deduction, and it should not look like one.

## 4. Six lookup states, and the two that change what you should believe

| `pinLookupData.source` | Treatment |
|---|---|
| assessor match, high confidence | `.kyp-src` provenance line — green tick, PIN, tax year |
| assessor match, `confidence === 'medium'` | **slate** `⚠ PIN matched — verify` + *"Confirm it is your parcel before relying on the figures."* Today this appends "(verify below)" to a **green** line; green plus a warning is a mixed signal. |
| **`geo_fallback`** | **red**, **above the hero blocks** — *"The figures below describe {nearestAddress}, the nearest parcel we could match — not this address."* |
| `exempt_api` | indigo — *"Assessment and tax figures will be absent or nominal — that is the record, not a gap in ours."* |
| no PIN | red + **the full input and Look Up**, and no section body until a PIN resolves |
| `propertyTaxData.error` | red — *"Nothing below is missing — it is unread."* Retry. Today the whole body is replaced by the raw error string. |

**`geo_fallback` is the one to fix first.** It means we could not find the parcel and are showing a
neighbouring building's record. Today it renders as muted grey with the full field grid beneath it at
normal weight, reading as though it describes the subject. It is the most consequential state in the
section and currently the quietest.

`coParcelPin` gets the same treatment and the same position — **above the grid**, not the amber strip
it uses today. Amber is not in the palette.

**Standing rule: where the figures describe something other than this address, say so above them.**
A caveat that follows the data has already been overtaken by it.

### 4.1 The PIN form inverts

A text input renders on **every** property today, including the ~95% where detection worked. Replace
it with the `.kyp-src` provenance line — PIN · source · tax year · `Refresh ⟳` · `Wrong parcel?
Enter a PIN →` · `Verify on Assessor site` · `Tax bill (Treasurer)` — and render the full form
**only** in the no-PIN state.

### 4.2 Manual entry renders only when something is missing

The callout currently fires whenever land **or** building sf is absent, which on a commercial parcel
is routine and produces a "details unavailable" panel beside a full income statement. Gate it on the
specific missing field, name that field, and keep everything entered labelled **manual** wherever it
appears. Preserve `manualBuildingSqFt`, `manualLandSqFt`, `manualStories` and the edit path.

`03`**. The takeaway line may *state* the disagreement — "the listing claims 3" — but the section
body is byte-identical whether or not a listing exists.
