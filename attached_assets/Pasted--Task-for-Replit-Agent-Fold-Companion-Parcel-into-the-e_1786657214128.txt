- **Do not rename the section.** It stays "Sales History." (The mockup's "SALES / ASSEMBLAGE" tags on the takeaway rows are annotation to show provenance — **do not ship those tags**.)

## Combined takeaway — keep the assemblage rows DETERMINISTIC (not LLM)
The assemblage rows carry safety-critical confidence language, so template **those rows** in code — don't hand them to the LLM. Fold them into the existing sales takeaway:
- **Headline** (updated to carry both): "{subject} and {companion} were bought together in {year} for ${price}, then split into separate names in {separated_year} — likely still one owner." (When not separated, drop the split clause: "{subject} and {companion} were bought together in {year} for ${price}, still under one owner.")
- **Keep the existing sales row(s)** (price history / hold period / last-arms-length) as they are.
- **Append (good):** acquired together {month year} — the ${price} was combined consideration for both {subject} and {companion} in one deed.
- **Append (caution, if separated):** split into {entities}; common control likely but not provable — **confirm identity**.
- **Append (neutral):** each lot carries its own single-PIN financing; no cross-collateralization; this parcel's Debt Snapshot is complete on its own.

When `is_assemblage` is false, the sales takeaway renders exactly as today — no appended rows, headline unchanged.
