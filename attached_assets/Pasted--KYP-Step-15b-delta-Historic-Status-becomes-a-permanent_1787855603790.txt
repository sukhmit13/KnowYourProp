# KYP — Step 15b **delta**: Historic Status becomes a permanent row

You have already given Replit Step 15b. **One decision changed after that**, when we settled on
flattening every section. Nothing else in 15b moved — the palette, the credits correction, the
demolition hold, the tier legend and the field inventory are all unchanged.

## What changed

**Then — §3 said conditional promotion.** A row only when the parcel is survey-rated, designated, or
in a landmark district. On the other ~97% of parcels no row existed at all, and the fact was a single
line inside `05 · Zoning & What You Can Build`.

**Now — it is a permanent top-level row.** Every parcel gets a `HISTORIC STATUS` row. On an unrated
parcel it renders **collapsed**, with the takeaway *"No historic designation on record."*, **no
badge**, and `.kyp-empty` in the body if opened.

Flat means flat. A conditional row is a special case with no payoff once every section is top-level,
and one collapsed line is cheaper than a rule nobody can see.

## What to change

1. **Remove the conditional gate.** The `historic` row always renders and always sits in
   `ACC_DEFAULT_ORDER`.
2. **The unrated state is a takeaway, not an absence.** `No historic designation on record.` in the
   row header, no badge.
3. **Do not add the line to `05 · Zoning & What You Can Build`.** If it was already added, remove it —
   the row carries it now, and the fact should not appear twice.
4. **Development Potential keeps its line** — *"A demolition permit here is held 90 days
   (§14A-4-407.6)"* — on rated parcels only. That one is deliberate duplication: it changes the answer
   in both places.

## Nothing to do for the CSS patch

The 15b patch you pasted carried 11 `.kyp-facts` rules I had included for Step 16. Step 16b no longer
uses them, so they sit in `kyp-base.css` unused. **Leave them.** They collide with nothing and a
later section may want a facts grid; removing them is churn for no gain.
