## 3. The row one-liner — the Step 24 bug, again

`scanBuilder.tsx:202` does this:

```js
if (ctx.peopleTakeaway?.takeaway?.title) dyn.people = { takeaway: mdEmph(ctx.peopleTakeaway.takeaway.title) };
```

and `accProps` prefers `scan?.takeaway` over `summary`. So the row currently reads:

> `25  NEIGHBORHOOD & PEOPLE — West Town's ZIP 60612 shows rising incomes and home values alongside…`

That is the AI headline, truncated by `mdEmph`'s 70-character clause cutter. Removing the takeaway
card alone will **not** fix it — the headline reaches the row through a different path. Prompt 1
removes `dyn.people` so the row falls back to the factual `SECTION_META.people.summary`.

## Two things the mock caught, so they don't recur

**A label inside a narrow bar clips.** The Chinese language bar is 2.5% wide and rendered as
`.5%` — `.kyp-hbar .htrack i` has `min-width:26px` and 7px of padding, which cannot hold four
characters. Below ~12%, put the count outside the fill using `.kyp-hbar .htrack > b.out` with
`style="left:{pct}%"`.

**Vote percentages do not fit inside the bar.** The Republican share is routinely 10–16%; a label
absolutely positioned at the bar's right edge lands on the light remainder in white text and
disappears. Percentages go in a `.kyp-votekey` row *under* the bar, D left, R right.
