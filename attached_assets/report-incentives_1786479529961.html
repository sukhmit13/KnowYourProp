SBIF — Small Business Improvement FundGrantAuthorized · TIF Open
SBIF is authorized in the Fullerton/Milwaukee TIF — it reimburses building improvements for commercial businesses on a grant basis.
Who can apply
  • Commercial businesses with up to $9M gross annual sales
  • Property owners with up to $9M net worth
  • Industrial businesses with up to 200 employees
Eligible improvements
  • Building renovations (windows, doors, roofing)
  • HVAC, electrical, plumbing upgrades
  • Interior remodeling & ADA improvements
National chains, franchises, and certain business types are not eligible.
