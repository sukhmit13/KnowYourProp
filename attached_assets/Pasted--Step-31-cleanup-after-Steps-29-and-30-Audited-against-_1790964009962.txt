## 1. The Simple / Detailed toggle is the wrong primitive — §Valuation

The one visible difference between the shipped calculator and the targets.

Four sites build the switch out of `.kyp-block`:

```
ValuationCalculator.tsx:399, 400   business Simple / Detailed
ValuationCalculator.tsx:494        tier Simple
ValuationCalculator.tsx:537, 538   tier Simple / Detailed
```

```tsx
<button type="button" className={`kyp-block ${tier === "simple" ? "ind" : "wash"}`} …>Simple</button>
```

`.kyp-block` is the **headline-statistic** primitive, sized for a 36px display numeral. Used as a
two-state switch it produces two large filled slabs where the targets have a compact segmented
control.

`grep -c 'kyp-seg' client/src/components/report/Valuation*.tsx` → **0**, while
`ProjectUseAnalysisSpine.tsx` and `DevelopmentSection.tsx` already use it. Same primitive here:

```tsx
<div className="kyp-segrow">
  <div className="kyp-seg">
    <button type="button" className={tier === "simple" ? "on" : ""}
      aria-pressed={tier === "simple"} onClick={() => setTier("simple")}>Simple</button>
    <button type="button" className={tier === "advanced" ? "on" : ""}
      aria-pressed={tier === "advanced"} onClick={() => setTier("advanced")}>Detailed</button>
  </div>
  <div className="spacer" />
  <div className="kyp-scopenote">{/* the one-line note opposite the control */}</div>
</div>
```

All four sites. Keep `aria-pressed`. Reference: `val-dc-target.html`, `val-mf-target.html`.

## 5. `AreaDetail.tsx` still renders a deleted takeaway

Not a Step 29 miss — §Area Detail was never in scope — but it is the same banned string surviving
on another surface. `client/src/pages/AreaDetail.tsx:600–608`:

```tsx
<Badge variant={deltaLevel === 'small' ? 'default' : deltaLevel === 'moderate' ? 'secondary' : 'destructive'}>
  {delta}%
</Badge>
…
<p className="text-sm text-muted-foreground">{enhancedData.deltaInterpretation}</p>
```

Two problems. `deltaInterpretation` is the prose takeaway the report already deleted. And
`variant="destructive"` paints a large labor-force delta red — a verdict color on a threshold this
product invented, which is exactly what the report version replaced with a slate-and-band
treatment.

Either apply the §17.2 pattern here, or drop the interpretation and keep the number. Do not leave
the red badge.
