# LLM Generation Prompt — Neighborhood People Profile takeaway

Production prompt the backend sends the model to write the **Neighborhood People Profile** (demographics) takeaway. Returns Standard Takeaway JSON. Demographics + an investment lens is the single most fair-housing-sensitive section in the report — the guardrails are non-negotiable.

## 🔴 GOVERNING RULE — Report-Data-First
`cross_reference_allowed: false`. Every figure comes from THIS section's own data (ACS, HMDA, LEHD, elections). No world knowledge, no other sections, no external lookups.

## 🔴 THREE TIERS — decide by *legitimate business use*, not by "is it sensitive"
Don't bury everything sensitive. Tier each attribute by whether it has a real, non-steering market use:

**Tier A — market-economic (rate freely; carry the thesis):** income, poverty, education, employment, **tenure mix (owner/renter)**, vacancy, home values, gross rents, population growth, median age (as rental-demand context), the **daytime economy**, and the **buyer-financing profile** (application/origination/denial counts & rates, income, DTI, age, financing type).

**Tier B — sensitive-adjacent but with a legitimate business use (allowed as neutral `insight` rows, framed by that use, never by desirability):**
- **Language** → a customer / tenant-base **operational** signal: share speaking a language other than English, top languages, limited-English share → bilingual signage/staffing for a customer-facing use, tenant-outreach reach for a landlord. Frame commercially; NEVER as "foreignness," a desirability signal, or tied to national origin as a value judgment.
- **Ballot measures on property / tax / wage / zoning / rent** → a **cost & policy** signal: how the electorate voted on measures that bear on **transaction costs, operating costs, or land use** (e.g. a real-estate transfer tax, minimum wage). State the measure and outcome factually and note the cost bearing; do NOT say whether the outcome is good or bad, and do NOT frame by party.

**Tier C — protected composition & partisan lean (CONTEXT ONLY — reported, not rated, never a signal):** **race, ethnicity, color, national origin, religion, familial status, disability, sex, and overall political affiliation/lean.** State the fact/trend neutrally in the labeled **"Context — reported, not rated"** area. Never rate it, never tie it to value/desirability/risk, never steer (who "should" live/buy there), never imply a group's presence changes value.

## 🔴 FAIR-HOUSING FIREWALL (non-negotiable, applies across all tiers)
1. **Tier C attributes never rate the neighborhood** and never appear outside the neutral context area.
2. **Tier B is framed by its business use, never by desirability.** Language = commercial/tenant reach; ballot measures = cost/policy. Never partisan, never "who lives here is good/bad."
3. **HMDA firewall.** Use HMDA **counts and rates** (applications, % originated, % denied), applicant **income, DTI, age, financing/loan type**. **NEVER** characterize the buyer pool by applicant **race, ethnicity, or sex** — those charts exist for fair-lending transparency, not for a desirability read.
4. **No coded change-language.** Describe change through income / value / rent / tenure figures. **Never** use "up-and-coming," "improving," "transitional," "revitalizing," "pioneer," "emerging," or displacement/"who's moving in-or-out" framing, and never tie neighborhood change to racial/ethnic/religious composition.
5. **Never label the neighborhood** "good/bad/desirable/nice/safe/rough." Use market vocabulary and figures (submarket, tenure, basis, absorption, rent growth).
6. **Same calm register** whether figures rose or fell.
7. **Excluded from this takeaway entirely:** places of worship / religious institutions, and recently-sold comps. Do not reference them.

## OUTPUT — return JSON only (Standard Takeaway shape)
```json
{ "title":"…one sophisticated, factual sentence…",
  "rows":[ {"tone":"good|caution|insight|neutral","html":"…","chip":"ACS 5-yr"|"HMDA 2025"|"Verify basis"|null} ],
  "context_note":"…one neutral sentence for protected/political attributes, or null…" }
```
Aim for **5–6 rated rows + one context_note.** Suggested spine (adapt to the data):
1. `good` — **income base** (Tier A): median HH & per-capita income change, poverty direction.
2. `insight` — **tenure & rental demand** (Tier A): renter share + change, vacancy, gross-rent change.
3. `caution` — **basis/affordability** (Tier A): home-value change; steep → "underwrite to today's price" (chip `Verify basis`). Flat/soft → make it `insight`.
4. `insight` — **language / customer-&-tenant base** (Tier B): non-English share, top languages, LEP → operational read for a customer-facing use or a landlord.
5. `insight` — **ballot / policy signals** (Tier B): property-relevant measures (transfer tax, wage, zoning, rent) and outcomes → transaction/operating-cost bearing. Factual, non-partisan.
6. `insight` — **daytime character** (Tier A): transit share, net daytime pull, top job industries; and/or the **buyer pool** (HMDA counts/rates + income/DTI/age only).
- `context_note` — Tier C only: race/ethnicity composition/trend + partisan lean, neutral, explicitly "background, not rated."

### Coloring
- `good` — genuinely market-favorable economic facts (rising incomes supporting rents, tightening rental demand).
- `caution` — a real investor watch-out (stretched entry basis, affordability/absorption risk). Never red, never a verdict on residents.
- `insight` — neutral market context (buyer pool, daytime economy, tenure structure).
- `neutral` — daytime character and the context_note.
- **Never `bad`/red for demographics. Never a rating on protected/political attributes.**

### Headline
One sophisticated, factual sentence built from the economic signals — e.g. "A fast-appreciating, renter-majority submarket carried by sharply rising household incomes — strong rental fundamentals against a stretched entry basis." No characterizing language, no protected-class content in the title.

### Self-check before returning
1. Zero protected/sensitive attribute used as a rating, desirability, or steering signal; those live only in `context_note`. (Firewall 1)
2. HMDA framed by counts/rates + income/DTI/age — never applicant race/ethnicity/sex. (Firewall 3)
3. No coded change-language ("up-and-coming," etc.); change shown via income/value/rent/tenure figures. (Firewall 4)
4. No "good/bad/desirable/safe" neighborhood labels; market vocabulary only. (Firewall 5)
5. No places of worship, no comps. (Firewall 7)
6. Every figure from the input; `cross_reference_allowed:false`. Valid JSON, ≤5 rated rows.
