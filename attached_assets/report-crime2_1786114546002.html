The Takeaway
Violent crime here is low — property crime is the more common issue, and it's falling.
Violent crime is the low end for Chicago — safer than 77% of community areas per capita (14.1 per 1,000). This is the number most buyers weigh, and it's well above the city middle.
Property crime is the more common concern, though still below average — safer than 58% (49.4 per 1,000). On the immediate block, the top type is motor-vehicle theft.
And the direction is good: total incidents are down 17% year-over-year (and down 14% since 2023).
