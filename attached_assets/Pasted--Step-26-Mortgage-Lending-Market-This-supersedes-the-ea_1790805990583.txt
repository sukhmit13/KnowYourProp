## 2. The rule that changed

**Scope and source notes go at the END of a block, once.** Not above the data, not between panels.

A *block* is a run of subsections under one control or one source — not each subsection. This
section has four blocks and therefore four footer lines:

| Block | Footer sits after | Class |
|---|---|---|
| 19.1–19.4 residential | Active Lenders | `.kyp-src` |
| 19.5 trends | the chart | `.ttfoot` (already there) |
| 19.6 comps | the comp cards | `.kyp-src` |
| 19.7–19.8 commercial | 7(a) lenders | `.kyp-src` |

**The one exception** is a fact that would cause a *misread of the numbers themselves*. That goes
in the subhead's counter-text (`.ct`), which costs no vertical space and is read on the way in:

| Subhead | `.ct` |
|---|---|
| 19.6 | `within 0.75 mi · class-matched` |
| 19.7 | `owner-occupied CRE · borrower, not property address` |
| 19.8 | `small business · borrower street address` |

Also delete `.chtitle` (`RunDetail.tsx:16136`). The y-axis, the x-axis year labels, the legend and
the four blocks above it all already say "transactions by type, 2022–2025".


  returns 503, and the panel shows *"Transaction data not available for this ZIP code."* The target
  shows the populated state because that is what the existing markup produces once the data
