## Why `.modelled` exists

On a Class 5 parcel the section prints NOI, cap rate, EGI, PGI, vacancy and expense ratio in the same
grid, at the same weight, as building size. Those are the **assessor's inputs to a valuation model**,
not the building's operating results — class-wide assumptions and an applied cap rate, not a trade.
`.modelled` makes that visible: dashed border, indigo wash, indigo labels, and a required `.kyp-modhd`
warning above the grid.

## These generalise components that already exist

`.kyp-tiles` is the zoning card's `MAX FAR / MAX HEIGHT` row and the corridor `.kyp-cxstat`.
`.kyp-recgrid` is the permit card's `PERMIT NO. / OWNER OF RECORD` block. `.kyp-yn` is the zoning
card's `✓ Residential / ✗ Industrial` list. Each is lifted into `kyp-base.css` so the County Record
uses the report's existing vocabulary instead of a new one. **Do not restyle the originals in this
step** — they keep their own rules until a later pass consolidates them.

## Why the grids are fixed-column

`.kyp-facts` defaults to `repeat(auto-fit,minmax(150px,1fr))`, which reflows to ragged rows whose
columns do not line up. Feedback on an earlier draft of this exact section was *"the older layout was
a little bit clearer."* `.g4` and `.g3` pin the columns; both collapse to 2 under 820px.
