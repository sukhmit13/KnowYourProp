## LOGIC (derived; compute in code)

### 1. Takeaway (moved to top, covers all three) — DERIVED, not free-form
Three bullets, each with a favorability dot class and a jump chip:
- **Bullet 1 — Day care (`.cn.c` caution when desert/underserved; `.cn.g` when adequate/plentiful).** State the tier word, the ratio, and the slot gap. Jump `#daycare`. The dot color follows the tier: desert/underserved → caution; adequate → neutral/`.i`; plentiful → good.
- **Bullet 2 — Elementary (`.cn.g` when the assigned school is Level 1/1+ and nearby schools are strong; downgrade to `.cn.i`/`.cn.c` if ratings are weak).** Name the assigned school + rating + distance, and the count within 1.5 mi. Jump `#elementary`.
- **Bullet 3 — High school (`.cn.i`).** If NO assigned high school, say so and note all N nearby are application/lottery with the closest; frame as "strong options, plan for the choice process." If there IS an assigned HS, mirror the elementary framing instead. Jump `#highschools`.
- Headline = one-line synthesis of the two-sided read (e.g. strong schools vs. tight daycare). Recompute per property — never hardcode "daycare is the weak spot"; if daycare is adequate/plentiful, the headline flips.

### 2. Day-care tier (drives the gauge + bullet 1)
- `ratio = children_under_5 / licensed_slots`. Tier by ratio: **> 3.0 Childcare desert**, **1.5–3.0 Underserved**, **1.0–1.5 Adequate**, **< 1.0 Plentiful**.
- The matching `.gs` segment and `.gl` label get `on`. Pill text + color: desert/underserved → caution styling (`.dpill`); adequate/plentiful → swap to a green pill (good) — the `on` fill and label colors follow suit (green, not marigold).
- `slot_gap` = slots needed to reach the Adequate threshold (ratio 1.5) − current slots, when underserved/desert; omit when already adequate+.
- Scope toggle re-scopes all daycare numbers (ZIP vs Neighborhood); keep the `.scopeline` "boundaries differ" note.

### 3. School ratings & favorability
- CPS SQRP levels: **Level 1+** (highest), **Level 1**, then Level 2/2+/3. **Level 1/1+ → green `.lvl`.** Level 2 → neutral; Level 3 → caution/red. Color the rating by quality; the `.lvl` chip in the reference is green because these are all 1/1+.
- **Access is categorical, not favorability:** `Boundary` → indigo `.acc.boundary`; `Open Enrollment` → neutral `.acc.enroll`. Never green/red — boundary vs lottery is a *type*, not good/bad.
- **Glance-card rating pill** (`.glrate`, green "Strong") reflects the *quality* of nearby schools. When a level has NO assigned school (high school here), keep the quality pill honest but ALSO render the amber `.glnote` "No assigned … all lottery." Don't let a green "Strong" imply guaranteed access.

### 4. Sort & the assigned school
- Both lists sort by **rating descending (1+ → 1 → 2 …), then distance ascending** within a rating.
- The **assigned (boundary) school** gets the `.assigned` star tag inline wherever it lands in the sort, AND is surfaced in the Elementary glance card's `.glassign` box. (Optional, if you prefer: pin the assigned school to the top of its list regardless of rating — pick one behavior and keep it consistent.)
- Grades: show the CPS grade range; append " · application / lottery" only for Open-Enrollment schools.
