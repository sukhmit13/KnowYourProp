## Rules that apply to all three prompts

1. **No takeaways.** Delete every sentence that tells the reader what the data means. Numbers,
   classifications from an outside authority, and sources stay. Judgements go.
2. **Scope and source notes go at the END of each subsection, once**, in a `.kyp-src`. Never above
   the data, never between panels.
3. **Chart forms do not change.** A line chart stays a line chart.
4. **Verdict colour needs an outside authority.** Green = good, orange = watch, red = bad,
   slate = context, indigo = no claim. If the cut points are ours, the footer says so.
   A rank is context and takes slate, never a verdict colour.
5. **Do not invent CSS.** Everything needed is in `kyp-base.css` plus the patch file.
6. **Do not label a chart with what the chart already draws.**

## Auto Repair / Auto Body — `:7411`

- `.kyp-bands b3` on `autoDependencyLevel` — low · moderate · high
- **Polarity note:** high auto dependency is *good* for an auto-service use and *bad* for a

  green/orange/red, and the bands show position only.
- Delete `"Strong demand for fuel sales"`, `"Moderate fuel demand"`,
  `"Limited market - high transit usage"` (`:7518–7522`) and
  `"Smaller vehicle market - consider location carefully"` (`:7535`). No replacement — they are the

Then **17.2 EV Registration Trends** (`:7834`). See `auto-target.html`.

Still a monthly line chart — the form does not change. Restyle axes and grid to `--kyp-*`; the
county series takes `var(--kyp-indigoL)`, the ZIP series `var(--kyp-orange)` in place of `#f4610a`.

**Split the one chart into two, side by side, each with its own axis.** Today both series share a
single `<YAxis>` (`:7902`), and the county runs in tens of thousands while the ZIP runs in

charts, same form, is the only option that loses nothing. Keep the two latest-value blocks above
them, which is where the levels actually belong.

The `"past 2 years"` caption (`:7869`) is a hardcoded claim about a window the data defines.

## Senior Care — `:7561`

Becomes **17.1 Senior Population**.

- `.kyp-blocks four`: `population65Plus` (+ `pct65Plus`), `seniorsLivingAlone`
  (+ `pctSeniorsLivingAlone`), `age85Plus`, `totalPopulation`
- Age breakdown 65–74 / 75–84 / 85+ as `.kyp-hbar` rows
- `rankDescription` becomes a slate `.chip.rank`, not a sentence
- `.kyp-bands b3` on `seniorDemandLevel` — **in slate, not in verdict colours**

> **Why slate.** 17.1 of the daycare target earns green/orange/red because it has both sides of the
> question: children *per licensed slot*. This panel counts demand with no supply beside it. A high
> senior share with twelve operators already inside a mile is not the same opportunity as the same
> share with none, and nothing in this panel can tell them apart. Colouring it would be the product
> making a call it has not earned. Same reasoning as auto dependency, different reason —
> there the polarity depends on the use; here the data is only half the question.
> The band names change from "Strong / Moderate / Limited demand" to "High / Moderate / Low share"
> for the same reason: the panel measures a share, not a demand.

**Delete the Market Assessment block (`:7719–7735`) entirely.**

`Estimated Addressable Market = seniorsLivingAlone + age85Plus * 0.5` (`:7733`). The `0.5` is not
sourced, not explained on screen, and not attributable to anyone outside this product — and the
result is rendered as a count of real people. `"Primary Target"` and `"Secondary Target"` are the
same two numbers already shown in the blocks above, relabelled as advice.

Keep the counts. Delete the arithmetic and the labels.

Also delete `"(Highest care needs)"` (`:7690`) — it is a judgement attached to a count.
