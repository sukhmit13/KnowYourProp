# KYP — Step 5d: The universal section contract + promote Crime to a top-level section

Two things. **Part A is a rule that applies to EVERY section** — it keeps getting fixed one section
at a time (Transit in 5a, News in 5b, now Crime) because it was never stated as a global contract.
Apply it as a sweep across all 14 sections, not just Crime. **Part B** promotes Safety & Crime out
of Proximity and makes it a top-level section.

---

# PART A — The section contract (apply to ALL sections)

## The row header is the ONLY title and the ONLY takeaway

Anatomy, exactly:

```
NN · SECTION TITLE — takeaway sentence · [verdict badge] · ⓘ · 👁 · ⌃
```

- **One title.** It lives in the row header. The body must NOT render a section title, eyebrow,
  `chead`, `.kyp-eyebrow`, old collapsible trigger, or any repeat of the section name.
- **One takeaway.** It lives in the row header, as body text at **15px** (`.kyp-acchd .tk`), one
  clause, ~90 characters. The body must NOT render a takeaway headline, a takeaway card, or a
  bullet list of takeaway rows.
- **One badge.** The single verdict badge in the row header. The body must NOT render a badge/pill
  row (the old collapsed-summary chips).
- The body starts **immediately with content** — the first thing after the header divider is a
  `kyp-subhead` or a data element.

## ❌ Never in a section body
1. A section title / eyebrow / `chead` / old `CollapsibleTrigger` (any repeat of the section name)
2. A large takeaway headline (any `font-size` above the 15px header takeaway)
3. A takeaway card (`.crm-take` / `take` lavender box) or its bullet rows
4. A status/badge chip row that duplicates the header badge
5. An outer card, border, or shadow wrapping the whole section
6. A nested collapsible that re-collapses the whole section

## Font sizes — locked, identical in every section
| Element | Size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Section title | 13.5px mono, uppercase | row header only |
| **Takeaway** | **15px, weight 500** | **row header only** |
| Subsection label (`kyp-subhead .lbl`) | 11px mono, uppercase | body |
| Body copy | 13–14px | body |

There is **no 20px+ headline inside any section body.** If a section renders its takeaway as a
large serif/sans headline in the body, that is the bug — move the text to the header and delete the
body copy.

## Takeaway length — must fit ONE line at desktop
The header takeaway shares its line with the section title, badge and three icons, so the practical
limit is **~70 characters**, not 90. A takeaway that wraps leaves an awkward orphan line — trim it
to the lead clause. Crime's generated headline ("Below-average crime for both violent and property
offenses, with reported crime trending down after a rise in 2024 — local incidents skew toward
property crime, led by theft.") becomes:

> **"Below-average on both counts, *trending down* after a 2024 rise."**

Apply the same trim wherever a section's generated takeaway is long. The full sentence stays
available to the Insight Report; only the header copy is shortened.

## Vertical rhythm — spacing above chart titles
`.kyp-charttitle` currently has only `8px` of top margin, so a chart title crowds whatever block
sits above it (visible where "Breakdown by type" jams against the crime count blocks), while
`.kyp-subhead` gets `26px`. Make the rhythm consistent:

```css
.kyp-charttitle{margin:24px 0 8px}   /* was 8px 0 6px */
```
This applies globally — every chart title in every section gets the same breathing room as a
subsection label.

## Sweep
Audit all 14 sections against the four "never" rules. Sections known to still violate: **Crime**
(below), and re-verify **News**, **Incentives**, **Transit** after their earlier fixes.

# PART B — Crime: fix the body + promote to a top-level section

## B1. Current state (from the live build)
The Crime body currently renders, before any content:
1. `AREA CRIME STATISTICS` — the old section title
2. A row of three green pills — `VIOLENT · SAFER THAN 58%` / `PROPERTY · SAFER THAN 62%` /
   `TREND ▼ 16% YOY` (the old `crm-badges` from the collapsed trigger)
3. `■ SAFETY & CRIME` — a second title/eyebrow
4. The takeaway as a **~34px headline** ("Below-average crime for both violent and property
   offenses…")

All four violate Part A. Delete all four from the body.

## B2. What Crime's row header becomes
```
08 · SAFETY & CRIME — Below-average crime, trending down after a 2024 rise. · [LOWER CRIME] · ⓘ 👁 ⌃
```
- Takeaway: `crimeTakeaway.headline`, trimmed to one clause at 15px. (The full generated sentence is
  too long for the header — trim to the lead clause; the detail is visible in the body.)
- Badge: one verdict badge from `crimeTier(violent.saferThanPercent)` — green ≥50 / orange 30–49 /
  red <30. The three old pills are **not** restored anywhere; their numbers already appear in the
  percentile tracks and trend chart in the body.

## B3. Crime body — first element is content
Straight into `kyp-subhead` "Around this address · trailing 12 mo", then the existing Step 4c
content unchanged: count blocks → breakdown bars → community-area subhead → percentile tracks →
year trend → source line. Nothing else added or removed.

## B4. Promote Crime to a top-level section
Crime is currently a **subsection of Proximity Details** — `RunDetail.tsx:3220` shows
`setSectionOpen('proximityDetails', true); setSectionOpen('crime', true)`, i.e. the parent must be
open to reach it. In the new architecture Safety & Crime is a **top-level section (08)**.

- Move the `#section-crime` block out of the Proximity Details parent so it is its own
  `<AccordionSection>` in the main sections list, at position 08 in the default order.
- It no longer depends on `proximityDetails` being open. Remove that coupling from the jump/menu
  action at `RunDetail.tsx:3220` (it should just open `crime`).
- Keep `id="section-crime"`, `id="crime-breakdown"`, `id="crime-area-ranking"`, and all
  `data-testid`s (`crime-status-badge-*`, `crime-radius-250ft`, `crime-radius-quarter`,
  `crime-area-ranking`, `crime-takeaway`) so deep links and print still resolve.
- Update `PRINT_SECTIONS` so crime is a top-level entry (not `level: 1` under Proximity) and it
  appears in the print list in its own right.
- Proximity & Amenities keeps its remaining subsections; it simply no longer contains crime.
