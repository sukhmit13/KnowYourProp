ADD VEHICLE OWNERSHIP DATA TO PROPERTY PAGES

Show vehicle ownership statistics for gas station, parking lot, 
auto repair shop, or other auto-dependent business analysis.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1: FIND VEHICLE OWNERSHIP DATA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Search Chicago Data Portal for:
https://data.cityofchicago.org/browse?q=american+community+survey

OR

https://data.cityofchicago.org/browse?q=vehicles

Look for datasets with:
- "Vehicles available" or "Vehicle ownership"
- By community area or census tract
- From American Community Survey (ACS)

Likely dataset:
"Census Data - Selected socioeconomic indicators"

Download and save to:
/server/data/demographics/vehicle_ownership.json

Data structure should include (by community area):
{
  "community_area": 22,
  "name": "Logan Square",
  "total_households": 35000,
  "households_no_vehicle": 5250,
  "households_1_vehicle": 14700,
  "households_2_vehicles": 10500,
  "households_3plus_vehicles": 4550,
  "avg_vehicles_per_household": 1.62,
  "pct_no_vehicle": 15.0,
  "pct_with_vehicle": 85.0,
  "commute_drive_alone_pct": 48.0,
  "commute_transit_pct": 35.0,
  "commute_walk_bike_pct": 12.0
}

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2: ADD TO PROPERTY PAGE DISPLAY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Show vehicle ownership data in demographics section or create new
"Auto Dependency" section.

const vehicleData = require('./data/demographics/vehicle_ownership.json');

function getVehicleOwnership(communityArea) {
  return vehicleData[communityArea] || null;
}

// On property page:
const ca = propertyData.community_area;
const vehicles = getVehicleOwnership(ca);

DISPLAY FORMAT:
──────────────

Auto Dependency:
────────────────

Vehicle Ownership:
- Average: {avg_vehicles_per_household} vehicles per household
- No vehicle: {pct_no_vehicle}% of households
- 1 vehicle: {pct_1_vehicle}% of households
- 2+ vehicles: {pct_2plus_vehicle}% of households

Commute Mode (to work):
- Drive alone: {commute_drive_pct}%
- Public transit: {commute_transit_pct}%
- Walk/bike: {commute_walk_bike_pct}%

Auto Dependency Level:
{IF avg_vehicles > 1.5}
  🚗🚗🚗 HIGH (above city average)
{ELSE IF avg_vehicles > 1.0}
  🚗🚗 MODERATE
{ELSE}
  🚗 LOW (transit-oriented area)
{END IF}

STEP 3: PROJECT-SPECIFIC INSIGHTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For certain project types, add auto dependency analysis:

IF project_type === "gas_station":
  Assessment for Gas Station:
  
  {IF avg_vehicles > 1.5 AND pct_drive > 50}
    ✅ STRONG DEMAND
    • High vehicle ownership ({avg_vehicles} per household)
    • Majority drive to work ({pct_drive}%)
    • Good market for fuel sales
  
  {ELSE IF avg_vehicles > 1.2}
    ⚠️ MODERATE DEMAND
    • Above-average vehicle ownership
    • Mixed transit/auto usage
    • Consistent but not peak demand
  
  {ELSE}
    🚩 WEAK DEMAND
    • Low vehicle ownership ({avg_vehicles} per household)
    • High transit usage ({pct_transit}%)
    • Limited market for fuel sales
  {END IF}

IF project_type === "parking_lot":
  Assessment for Parking Facility:
  
  {IF pct_no_vehicle > 20}
    ⚠️ Consider carefully
    • {pct_no_vehicle}% of households have no vehicle
    • High transit usage in area
    • May face low utilization
  
  {ELSE IF avg_vehicles > 1.5}
    ✅ Good potential
    • High vehicle ownership
    • Likely parking demand
  {END IF}

IF project_type === "auto_repair":
  Assessment for Auto Repair Shop:
  
  Vehicle ownership: {avg_vehicles} per household
  Total vehicles in area (est.): {total_households × avg_vehicles}
  
  {IF avg_vehicles > 1.3}
    ✅ Good market
  {ELSE}
    ⚠️ Limited market
  {END IF}

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DATA NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SOURCE: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS)
- 5-year estimates (most stable)
- By community area
- Updated annually

UPDATE FREQUENCY:
- Download new ACS data annually when released
- Typically released in December for previous year

CALCULATIONS:
- Avg vehicles per household = total vehicles / total households
- Commute mode from ACS Table S0801 (Commuting Characteristics)
- Vehicle availability from ACS Table B25044 or B08201
