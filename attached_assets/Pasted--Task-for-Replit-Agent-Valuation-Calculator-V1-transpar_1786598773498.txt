# Task for Replit Agent — Valuation Calculator V1: transparent NOI + Simple/Advanced + mode scaffold

## Why (the bug this fixes)
NOI is currently a single typed number ("Net — taxes & insurance already deducted"). It nets **only taxes and insurance** and skips vacancy, management, repairs, utilities, and reserves — which **overstates NOI and inflates every downstream metric.** On 1551 W Division it produces a **15% cap rate** (no West Town 4-flat caps that high — it's the missing expenses). V1 makes NOI a transparent, editable build-up so the number is trustworthy, and adds the two-knob structure (mode / tier) with Investor mode built.

Visual targets (render these): **report-calc-integrated.html** (placement + flow), **report-calc-tiers.html** (Simple/Advanced), **report-noi-transparent.html** (the full statement). Reuse the app's existing card CSS.

## Scope of V1
- **Investor / buy-to-lease mode only.** Build the transparent NOI. Owner-occupant/SBA and Tenant/leasing modes are **V2** (separate spec) — but scaffold the mode control now (below).
- **Simple + Advanced tiers**, which are the collapsed/expanded views of ONE model (not two calculators).

## 1. Placement & order — build before judge
Insert the income step AND reorder so NOI is built **before** the verdict that depends on it:
```
1 Enter the Deal   2 Income → NOI (NEW)   3 Deal at a Glance   4 Coverage Check   5 How it's Calculated
```
🔴 **Income → NOI comes before Deal at a Glance.** A verdict (cap rate / DSCR) must not render before the NOI it's built from. Flow reads: inputs → NOI built → verdict → coverage → reference. (This corrects a build where the Glance sat at Step 2, ahead of the income breakdown.)

**Step 1 = Purchase + Financing only — no income column at all.** Remove income from Step 1 entirely (no NOI Basis dropdown, no computed NOI, no forward pointer). Reason: keeping a rent-basis input in Step 1 duplicates the rent — it would appear both there and as the first line of the Step 2 statement. One source only.
- The **rent basis lives as the first line of the Step 2 income statement**: the Residential-rent line carries the basis selector inline — `Residential rent [Market ▾ · 4 × $3,195/mo] = $153,360`. Market-vs-actual and units×rent are chosen there, where the number is used.
- Step 1 becomes a clean two-column "the deal": **Purchase** (price) and **Financing** (loan type, rate, down/term). Give the two columns room; two tight inputs read cleaner than a false-symmetry third column that points elsewhere.
- The NOI number first appears in Step 2, where it's built, then flows to Step 3's Glance. (Manual override still lives in Step 2 Advanced: see §5.)

## 2. The transparent NOI model (the core)
NOI is built, never typed:
```
INCOME
  Residential rent          units × rent (from parcel; market or actual per NOI Basis)   [editable]
  Commercial rent           ONLY if property class is mixed/commercial (see §4)          [editable]
  Other income              parking/laundry/storage — optional                           [editable]
  − Vacancy & credit loss   default 5% residential / 10% commercial                      [editable]
  = Effective Gross Income (EGI)
OPERATING EXPENSES  (ALL present, ALL editable, each source-tagged)
  − Property taxes          [from record]
  − Insurance               [estimate — clearly labeled editable]
  − Property management     default 6% of EGI (imputed even if self-managed)
  − Repairs & maintenance   [estimate]
  − Utilities (owner-paid)  [estimate]
  − Replacement reserves    default $250/unit/yr
  = Total operating expenses
NOI = EGI − Total operating expenses
```
- **Source tags on every line:** `from record` (taxes), `estimate · edit` (insurance, R&M, utilities), `from parcel` (rents), `assumption · edit` (vacancy, management, reserves).
- Editing any line recomputes NOI (and everything downstream) live.

## 3. Simple vs Advanced — same model, different dials (TIER)
A header toggle. **Both tiers use the identical full-expense model.**
- **Simple (default):** Step 3 renders **collapsed** — the NOI result + a chip row of the assumptions used ("taxes from record · insurance est · 5% vacancy · 6% mgmt · R&M · reserves") + an "Edit assumptions → Advanced" link. No per-line editing.
- **Advanced:** Step 3 renders **expanded** — the full statement, every line editable.
- 🔴 **Simple must never revert to "rent − taxes − insurance."** It is the full model with lines hidden behind defaults, not a lighter calculation. The NOI is identical in both tiers.

## 4. Auto-detect income lines from property composition
Do NOT ask the user the property type — infer it:
- **Cook County property class** (primary) + parcel data → residential / mixed / commercial.
  - Residential class → residential rent line only, **no commercial line**.
  - Mixed/commercial class → **add** the Commercial rent line.
- **Zoning is NOT the composition source** (it says what's *allowed*, not what *exists*) — use it only for a validation flag.

## 5. Mode indicator (V1 = Investor only; do NOT show a disabled 3-way)
V1 builds **Investor / buy-to-lease** only.
- 🔴 **Do NOT render a segmented mode control with Owner-occupant/Tenant greyed out as "coming soon"** — a 3-way selector with two disabled options looks unfinished in production. Instead show a **single static indicator: "Analyzing as: Investor · buy-to-lease."** Reintroduce a real selector in V2 when the other modes are actually built.
- **Tier control (Simple/Advanced) stays** as a live toggle in the header.
- **Defaults:** mode is Investor (only option now); **tier defaults from funnel completeness** (filled the deal inputs → Advanced; skipped → Simple). Show a one-line "From your project type: … · change the view" and let the tier toggle override in one click.
- **NOI manual override (Advanced only):** allow "enter NOI directly" as an escape for a user pasting a real T-12 — but render a clear **"manual — not from the lines"** state. Default is the computed build-up.

## 6. 🔴 One NOI, everywhere
NOI is computed **once** in Step 3 and read by: Step 1's Operating Income summary, Deal at a Glance (cap rate, DSCR, cash flow), Coverage Check ("Your NOI"), and How it's Calculated. They must **never** show different NOIs. (Same one-resolved-value discipline as the debt-snapshot work.)

## 7. Two DSCR conventions — show both
Lenders measure debt coverage two ways; show both so a user isn't blindsided when a broker quotes a different number. Visual target: **report-dscr-both.html**.
- **Economic DSCR** = `NOI ÷ annual debt service` (full operating expenses). The honest, true-cash-flow read; how commercial / portfolio / 5+ unit lenders underwrite. **This is the primary/headline DSCR** in Deal at a Glance. Typical floor 1.25×.
- **DSCR-loan ratio** = `gross market rent ÷ PITIA`, where **PITIA = P&I + property taxes + insurance + HOA**. Skips vacancy, management, and reserves; what a **1–4 unit non-QM DSCR-loan** lender quotes. Typical floor 1.0–1.25×.
- **Deal at a Glance:** DSCR tile shows the **economic** number as primary (labeled "Economic — full operating expenses"), with the **DSCR-loan ratio beneath** as a secondary line ("DSCR-loan (1–4 unit): 2.04 · rent ÷ PITIA").
- **Coverage Check (Step 4):** show both conventions spelled out — name, who-uses-it tag, the formula with real values, the result, and its floor/pass — plus a one-line "both clear / here's which is the term-sheet number" summary.
- 🔴 **One source, two ratios:** PITIA's taxes & insurance reuse the **same values from the NOI statement** (§2) — don't re-enter or re-estimate them. Gross market rent = the residential-rent line before vacancy. Both ratios recompute live with the inputs.
- Show the DSCR-loan ratio only for property types where the product applies (1–4 unit); for larger/commercial, economic DSCR alone is fine (note the product doesn't apply).

## 8. Flags (surface, don't block)
- **Property taxes may reassess on sale** — Cook County resets assessed value on transfer; the current bill can understate a buyer's taxes. Note on the taxes line.
- **Unusually high cap rate** — if cap rate exceeds ~10%, show "verify your rent and expenses — a cap this high usually means an assumption is off." (This is what would have caught 1551.)
- **Rent basis** — label whether residential rent is *market* or *actual* (reuse the existing NOI Basis control).

## 9. Do NOT (V1)
- Do not build Owner-occupant/SBA or Tenant/leasing math (V2) — scaffold the control only.
- Do not change the structure of Deal at a Glance / Coverage Check / How it's Calculated — they consume NOI unchanged; they just receive the corrected (lower, honest) number.
