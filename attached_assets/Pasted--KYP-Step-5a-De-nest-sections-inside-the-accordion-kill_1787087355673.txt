# KYP — Step 5a: De-nest sections inside the accordion (kill the old chrome)

**Problem (visible on Transit Access, applies to every section):** the AccordionSection wrapper is
wrapping each section's OLD card whole. Expanding a row reveals: (1) the old bordered
section box with its OWN title bar and its OWN expand arrow, (2) the old lavender "Takeaway" box
repeating the takeaway the row header already shows, and (3) old chip-summary sub-collapsibles
("CTA RAIL STATIONS (2 MI) ▶") that need a THIRD click. That's three layers of chrome and two
takeaways. The wrapper must REPLACE the old chrome, not contain it.

## The rule (apply to every section in the accordion)

When a section lives inside `<AccordionSection>`, its body may contain **content only**:

1. **DELETE the old outer card/box** (`Card`, `.seccard`, `.tz-*` wrappers, borders, shadows,
   padding shells). The accordion row *is* the card. Inside: no perimeter borders at all.
2. **DELETE the old section title bar** (the "■ TRANSIT ACCESS & RIDERSHIP ▾" header, `chead`,
   `CollapsibleTrigger`, chips-when-collapsed summary, and its expand arrow). One expand control
   exists: the accordion row's chevron. Nothing inside the body collapses the whole section again.
3. **DELETE the old takeaway box** (`.crm-take` / `take` lavender card — label, serif title, AND
   its bullet rows). The takeaway lives in the row header, once. The visuals carry the detail.
   (If a bullet contains data that exists nowhere else, keep the DATA by moving it into the
   relevant subsection — but no lavender box, no bullet list at top.)
4. **DE-COLLAPSE the sub-blocks.** Old inner collapsibles ("CTA Rail Stations", "L Ridership",
   "Metra", "Bus Routes", "Street Traffic", etc.) render OPEN, always, with a `kyp-subhead`
   divider instead of a clickable chip-row header:
   `<div className="kyp-subhead fam-indigo"><span className="lbl">CTA Rail Stations</span><span className="ct">2 mi</span><span className="rule"/></div>`
   Remove their ▶ toggles and their collapsed chip summaries (the chips' info — counts, nearest —
   is already in the content). ONE collapse level per report: the section row. Nothing nested.
5. **Indent, don't border.** The body uses the accordion's established indent
   (`.kyp-accbody` padding + the section's colored left rail) as the only structure. No inner
   boxes-in-boxes; subsections separated by `kyp-subhead` rules and whitespace.
6. Keep ALL data, charts, testids, ids. This is chrome removal only — zero data changes.

## Header-row layout fix (applies to every accordion row)
The current header breaks on long takeaways: title and takeaway are separate flex items, so the
sentence wraps as a dropped block ("— …" starting a new line under the title) and the right-side
stat gets vertically centered INTO the sentence ("…bus route at 5 RAIL STOPS the door"). Fix:

```css
.kyp-acchd{align-items:flex-start}
.kyp-acchd .drag{margin-top:6px}
/* title + takeaway = ONE inline sentence that wraps naturally under itself */
.kyp-acchd .mid{flex:1;min-width:0;line-height:1.5;padding-top:3px;display:block}
.kyp-acchd .eb{display:inline;white-space:normal}
.kyp-acchd .tk{display:inline}
.kyp-acchd .tk::before{content:"— ";color:var(--kyp-muted);margin:0 4px 0 8px;font-weight:400}
/* right cluster pins to the FIRST line, never mid-sentence */
.kyp-acchd .badge,.kyp-acchd .ico{margin-top:2px}
```
(i.e. `.mid` is a normal text block with the eyebrow and takeaway as inline spans — the em-dash
comes from `.tk::before`, so takeaway strings must NOT start with their own dash.)

**REMOVE the floating stat from header rows entirely** (the "5 / RAIL STOPS", "6.2% / CAP",
"12 / COMPS" mini-blocks). It's redundant — the takeaway sentence carries the key number and the
badge carries the verdict — and it's what causes the mid-sentence collisions. Header anatomy is
exactly: **number · TITLE — takeaway · verdict badge · ⓘ · eye · chevron.** Nothing else. Delete
the `stat` prop/markup from the AccordionSection header and from the section metadata. If a
section's key number isn't in its takeaway sentence, write it into the sentence.

Also keep header takeaways SHORT — one clause, ~90 characters max. Transit's becomes:
**"Strong access, with Metra a *3-minute walk* and a top-10 bus route at the door."**
If a section's takeaway runs long, trim it for the header; the detail lives in the body.
