FAQ
Q: Why should I get a report BEFORE making an offer? A: You're about to put down $5,000-$50,000+ in earnest money and hire an attorney ($1,500-$3,000). If you discover major issues after you're under contract, you've already spent money and committed to the deal. Getting a report first lets you know the full picture BEFORE you risk your money, and gives you negotiating leverage if issues exist.
Q: Can I use this information to negotiate? A: Absolutely! Many buyers use our reports to negotiate price reductions. For example: "I see there's a $20K lien on the property. I'm adjusting my offer to reflect that." Or "The property needs special use zoning approval, which costs $15K+. Let's discuss price." Knowledge is leverage.
Q: Is this a replacement for a title company? A: No. We provide pre-purchase due diligence to help you evaluate properties before making an offer. You'll still need a title company at closing for title insurance and final lien clearance.
Q: How accurate is the lien search? A: We achieve 75%+ coverage by searching both property PIN and owner name on the Cook County Recorder. However, some liens may be recorded elsewhere or pending recording. Always verify with a title company before closing.
Q: Can you tell me if my property is "definitely eligible" for tax incentives? A: We identify which location-based incentive zones your property falls within (Opportunity Zones, TOD areas, etc.). Final eligibility depends on your specific situation, project type, and transaction structure. Consult with a tax advisor for definitive guidance.
Q: What areas do you cover? A: Currently, we cover all of Chicago and Cook County, Illinois. We're expanding to New York City and Philadelphia soon, with more markets coming in 2026.
Q: How current is the data? A: We pull data from public sources daily. Most data is current within 24-48 hours. Property sales and permit data may have a 30-90 day lag depending on recording/filing timelines.
Q: What if I find a lien that the seller didn't disclose? A: This is exactly why you order a report BEFORE making an offer. If you discover undisclosed liens, you can: (1) walk away before risking earnest money, (2) negotiate a price reduction to account for the lien, or (3) require the seller to clear it before closing. Knowledge protects your investment.
Q: Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied? A: Yes. If you're not satisfied with your report, contact us within 7 days for a full refund, no questions asked.
Q: Do you offer bulk pricing for agents or developers? A: Yes! A monthly subscription makes the most sense for real estate professionals.
