# KYP — Step 5b: News section fixes (de-nest + takeaway placement + logo size)

Follow-up to Step 5a. The News section still shows old chrome and a mis-placed takeaway. Four fixes.

## 1. Delete the duplicate section title in the body
The row header already reads **"14 · NEWS ON THIS PROPERTY — …"**. The body then renders another
**"■ NEWS"** title bar. Delete it (Step 5a rule #2 — it wasn't applied to this section). One title
per section, in the row header only.

## 2. The takeaway belongs in the ROW HEADER, not the body
Currently the header shows a generic description ("Any news coverage that names this specific
property") while the real AI takeaway sits inside the body under the momentum cards, with its
bullet rows.

- **Header takeaway** = the actual generated takeaway, trimmed to one clause (~90 chars). For this
  run: *"Edgewater's news cycle pairs a lively dining scene with steady development activity."*
  Source it from `nnTakeaway.takeaway.title` / `newsTakeaway.section.title` (whichever scope leads),
  falling back to the static description only when no takeaway exists.
- **DELETE from the body**: the takeaway headline AND its bullet list (the "High momentum reading /
  Pipeline taking shape / Permit records may lag the news / Culture and retail signal" rows) — this
  is the `.crm-take` / `take` block that Step 5a rule #3 removes. The visuals + article rows carry it.
- **KEEP** any hard datum that lived ONLY in a bullet by moving it into the relevant subsection
  (e.g. the "permit records may lag the news / matched-once" caveat becomes a `kyp-src`-style note
  under the Development list, not a bullet at the top).

## 3. Logo tiles are too large
`.kyp-archlogo` is ~112px (146px on lead rows) — it dominates one-line headlines. Reduce:

```css
.kyp-archlogo{width:72px;height:72px;padding:10px;border-radius:10px}
.kyp-archrow.lead .kyp-archlogo{width:88px;height:88px}
.kyp-archthumb{width:72px;height:72px;padding:9px 10px}      /* fallback plate matches */
.kyp-archthumb .tsrc{font-size:14px}
.kyp-archrow{padding:16px 0;gap:16px}                         /* tighten with the smaller tile */
```
The logo is a wayfinding accent, not the hero — the headline is.

## 4. Restore the two labeled subsections (the merge from Step 4d)
The body currently opens straight into Neighborhood-News content (momentum cards + its takeaway)
with no subsection label, even though the section is "News on This Property". Restore the specced
structure:

- `kyp-subhead` **"Site-Specific Coverage"** + count → the parcel/adjacent archive rows (with the
  record-check status dot).
  **Empty state (important):** when there is no coverage naming this parcel, still render the
  subhead — with the count as `0` — followed by exactly this line and nothing else:

  ```tsx
  <div className="kyp-empty">No news coverage names this property.</div>
  ```
  ```css
  .kyp-empty{font-size:13.5px;color:var(--kyp-ink2);padding:14px 0 4px;line-height:1.5}
  ```
  Do NOT hide the subsection, do not substitute neighborhood articles, and do not leave it blank.
  An explicit "none" is a real finding (clean record) — silence reads like a bug.
  When Site-Specific is empty, the **row-header takeaway** falls through to the Neighborhood-News
  takeaway (as in the current Edgewater example).
- `kyp-subhead` **"Neighborhood News"** + `{communityArea} · 120 days` → the momentum + article-count
  blocks, then the **Entertainment & Culture** and **Real Estate & Development** lists under
  `kyp-cath` headers (stage tags + "counted once in Corridor" cross-link preserved).

Momentum block colors stay: score ≥75 green, ≥50 orange, else red; article count = slate (a count,
not a verdict).
