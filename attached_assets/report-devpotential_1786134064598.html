The Takeaway
Over-built and grandfathered — you've inherited more density than today's zoning would allow, though further expansion is capped.
Built to 267% of the zoning limit — FAR is 2.40 vs a 0.9 max. On paper it exceeds current zoning.
But that excess is grandfathered. A new project here couldn't be approved for this much floor area — inherited over-build is often an asset, giving you density a fresh build can't get.
The trade-off is flexibility. Adding floor area or units needs a variance; zoning otherwise caps it at 1 unit (RS-3) and an ADU isn't as-of-right.
