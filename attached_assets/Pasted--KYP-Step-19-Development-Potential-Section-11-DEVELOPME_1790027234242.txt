## ⚠️ 0. Read this before anything else

**This spec was written without the source.** Every other step in this series was audited against
`RunDetail.tsx`. This one was built from **seven screenshots of the live build 2026-09-01**, covering
100% of what renders on 1126 W Bryn Mawr. Two consequences:

**I do not know the field names.** Where this spec says `median asking rent, 2-bed, 0.75 mi` it means
*the value that renders at that position today* — find the variable that produces it and keep using
it. Do not create new state, and do not re-derive anything the code already computes.

**I cannot see conditional branches.** The screenshots show one property: under-built, no commercial
listings, RentCast and Airbnb both populated. Nothing here describes what happens when the lot size
is missing, the property is **over-built**, FAR is unavailable for the district, or a feed returns
nothing. §9 lists the branches that must be checked; treat it as a to-do, not a description.

**The six commercial listings in the mock are invented.** LoopNet returned nothing for 60660, so
there was no real data to draw. The addresses, sizes and rates are fabricated so the populated layout
could be reviewed. **Do not hardcode them, and do not treat the rates as reference values.** The
design is the deliverable there; the data is not.

## 1. What the section is today

Seven blocks, each its own `<Collapsible>`: a `Rental Economics` summary card pair, `FAR Analysis`,
`Fair Market Rent (HUD FY2026)`, `Real-Time Rental Data`, `Short-Term Rental Market (Airbnb)`,
`Commercial Lease Market (LoopNet)`, `Hourly Space Market (Peerspace)`.

Five defects, all visible in the screenshots:

**The same comparison renders twice.** `Fair Market Rent` shows five FMR cards; `Real-Time Rental
Data` then shows a `vs FMR` column against those same figures. One subject, two places, 300px apart.

**The summary card sets up a comparison and abandons it.** `Rental Economics` puts long-term
`$1,895/mo` beside short-term `~$22,667/yr` — monthly against annual. The reader is invited to
compare and handed incomparable units.

**`+3,459 sq ft` appears three times** — the bar legend, the `Additional Buildable` card, and
`Remaining FAR: 0.31` in the meta strip.

**The Airbnb table mixes denominators.** `Studio 9 · 6.4%` through `4+ Bedrooms 9 · 6.4%` are
percentages of **140**; `Private Room 32 · 18.5%` and `Shared Room 1 · 0.6%` are percentages of
**173**. Two bases in one column. Separately, the bedroom rows sum to **117** under a header reading
`140 LISTINGS` — 23 listings vanish silently.

**`Shared Room · $0/nt · 99%`.** A null rendered as a price, beside an occupancy figure derived from
one listing's availability calendar.

## 2. Structure

Four numbered subheads. **Every subsection opens with a three-block row**, so the section can be read
from the twelve blocks alone.

| | Subhead | Context slot |
|---|---|---|
| — | *(section blocks)* | envelope: unused sf · ceiling · height cap |
| .1 | `THE BUILDABLE ENVELOPE` | `FAR {far} of {max} · {n} sq ft unused` |
| .2 | `REAL-TIME RENTAL MARKET` | `RentCast · {n} listings in {zip} · {m} within 0.75 mi` |
| .3 | `SHORT-TERM RENTAL (AIRBNB)` | `Inside Airbnb · {n} listings · {neighborhood}` |
| .4 | `COMMERCIAL LEASE MARKET` | `LoopNet · {n} for-lease listings · within {r} mi` |

Numbers come from the accordion's live `index` via `SectionNumberContext` — **never hardcode `04`**.
They renumber over the subheads that actually render.

**Name the feed, not the content.** `.2` is `REAL-TIME RENTAL MARKET` because RentCast is a live feed
and HUD FMR is an annual static table; the merged table in `.2` carries both and the reader has to be
able to tell them apart. Same reason the FMR column header reads `HUD FMR · FY2026` and the market
overview header carries `· LIVE`.

## 3. Section blocks — the envelope

Reuse `.kyp-blocks` / `.kyp-block`. No `.bclaim` chips; nothing here is a seller claim.

| # | Class | `.bv` | `.bl` | `.bd` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `grn` | `{buildableSf}` + `<u>sf</u>` | `Unused floor area` | % of envelope built, and that no rezoning is needed |
| 2 | `ind` | `{maxTotalSf}` + `<u>sf</u>` | `The zoning ceiling` | lot sf × FAR, and the height cap |
| 3 | `dark` | `{maxHeightFt}` + `<u>ft</u>` | `Height cap` | storeys, and that it is a second cap independent of FAR |

**Block 1 is green because unused FAR is the finding the section exists to report.** Blocks 2 and 3
are limits, not verdicts — indigo and black.

⚠️ **Height gets a block.** It was buried in the meta strip. On many Chicago lots the height cap binds
before FAR does, and a reader who only sees `13,500 sq ft` will over-estimate what fits.

## 4. `.1` The buildable envelope

**The FAR bar carries its own labels.** `.kyp-farbar` is a two-segment bar: indigo `BUILT · FAR {x}`,
green `+{y} FAR`. The percentage sits above it in Instrument Serif. The separate legend row
(`■ Built FAR 0.89  ■ Available headroom…`) is **cut** — the bar says it.

**Six facts in `.kyp-recgrid.inline`**, three across, two rows, label left and value right on one
line: Current FAR · Max allowed FAR · **Additional buildable** (indigo cell) · Existing building ·
Max height · Lot size.

This replaces both the four centred cards and the meta strip beneath them. `Remaining FAR: 0.31` is
**cut** — it is the green bar segment restated.

**What counts toward FAR** keeps all three rules, as `.kyp-yn` ✗/✗/✓ rows. The heading is cut; the
rows read without it. A fourth row carries the zoning-attorney line and the point that
**the buildable figure is an envelope, not a permit**.

## 5. `.2` Real-time rental market

### 5.1 Blocks

`ind` median 2-bed asking rent · `grn` percent over the HUD baseline · `dark` median days to lease.
The green block is the only verdict in the subsection and the words carry it.

### 5.2 The long-term vs short-term strip — `.kyp-vs`

This is what the current `Rental Economics` card should have been. One unit, one period, and the
delta stated:

| Gross rent per year | Long-term | Short-term | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Bedroom | `{ltrMo} × 12` | `{nightly} × {nightsBooked}` | signed, plus a plain-word verdict |

**Render a row only where both feeds report that bedroom count.** If fewer than two rows survive,
do not render the strip at all — a comparison with one side missing is worse than no comparison.

Each figure shows its derivation beneath it (`$1,895/mo × 12 · 136 listings`), including the listing
count the median rests on.

⚠️ **Pick one derivation for short-term annual revenue.** The current build computes it twice and
disagrees with itself: the summary card shows `~$22,667` (rate × occupancy × 365) and the table
reports 165 booked nights (rate × nights = `$22,770`). Use **rate × booked nights** and delete the
other path.

### 5.3 The merged rent table

FMR folds in as a column. Six columns: bedroom · `HUD FMR · FY2026` · ZIP median · within 0.75 mi ·
`vs FMR` · median sf. The five standalone FMR cards, the ZIP-vs-Chicago gradient meter, and the
`Why FMR matters for underwriting` box are all **cut** — see §7.

`vs FMR` renders as a `.vd` chip, green above the baseline and orange below. The market-overview
table (median rent, average rent, days on market) follows as a second small table.

**2-bedroom quality tiers** keep all four bands and the scale, restyled to `.kyp-tier`. The label is
a `.kyp-subhead.sub` — a sub-label, not a numbered subhead.

## 6. `.3` Short-term and `.4` Commercial

### 6.1 Short-term blocks and table

`ind` median 2-bed nightly rate · `dark` 2-bed occupancy · `ind` implied gross per year.

The table gains an **implied /yr** column — rate × booked nights, per row. Every value is computed
from two numbers already in the table; nothing new is fetched.

**Fix the denominators.** Drop the share percentages entirely and show raw counts. Counts carry the
same information and cannot be read against the wrong base. Add an explicit
`Bedroom count not reported — {n}` row so the entire-home column reconciles to its header.

**`Shared room`** renders `no price listed` rather than `$0/nt`, dimmed with the room rows.

**Seasonality survives** as a strip under the table: peak months, slow months, blended occupancy.
This is currently the last line of the section and is easy to lose; for short-term underwriting a
49% annual occupancy that is 80% in July and 20% in February is a different asset.

### 6.2 Commercial blocks and table

`ind` median retail asking $/sf/yr · `dark` spaces available in radius · `ind` **distance to the
nearest available space**, naming it.

Then one table: space · use · size · asking · lease type · distance. **No roll-up strip** — with a
handful of listings, a summary of the rows directly above them is duplication.

⚠️ The nearest-space block is the one to get right. The closest comparable lease is the single most
useful thing this feed produces and it is currently buried mid-list.

### 6.3 Peerspace is deleted

`Hourly Space Market` comes out entirely — the subsection, the `useJBANearby`/Peerspace hook wiring
if it serves nothing else, the source-line clause and the `Search Peerspace Chicago →` link.

## 8. Prose rule

**A sentence earns its place only by naming a source, defining a unit, or stating a limit. If it
restates a number already on screen, cut it.**

Three notes survive the section, none longer than 45 words: both rent columns are *gross* and how
each was derived · HUD FMR is a conservative baseline, not a forecast · Airbnb rates are what hosts
*ask* and occupancy comes from `availability_365`. Plus one `.kyp-method` on how the envelope is
figured, and one `.kyp-src`.

Do not add a takeaway, a verdict paragraph, or an interpretation of a delta the table already shows.

## 9. Branches I could not see — check these

- **Over-built** (`FAR ≥ max`). Block 1 must not read `Unused floor area` with a negative. Expect
  `orange`, `0 sq ft`, and wording that says the envelope is used up.
- **No lot size or no district FAR** — the whole of `.1` depends on it. Fall back to two blocks, then
  one full-width; never one block at a third width.
- **A feed returns nothing** — RentCast, Airbnb or LoopNet. Follow the Step 9c `not_found` pattern:
  render no body, pass `collapsible={false}`, indigo `NONE ON RECORD` badge, and a takeaway that
  states what was searched.
- **Bedroom coverage gaps** — `4 Bedrooms` already shows `—` for the 0.75 mi column. Any derived
  column must tolerate a missing operand rather than printing `$NaN`.
- **The `Efficiency` / `Studio` merge.** HUD labels it *Efficiency*; RentCast labels it *Studio*. The
  merged table puts them on one row. Confirm that is the same unit type before shipping.

## 10. CSS — `kyp-base-patch-19.css`, 49 rules, **provisional**

Written blind. **Before appending, grep `kyp-base.css` for every selector in the patch and delete any
that already exists** — an append at equal specificity silently overrides the live rule and would
regress sections already shipped.

26 rules were deliberately left out for that reason (`.kyp-blocks`, `.kyp-block` and its colour
variants, `.kyp-subhead` and its children, `.kyp-recgrid` base, `.kyp-yn`, `.kyp-note`,
`.kyp-method`, `.kyp-src`). The patch header lists them.

**New families:** `.kyp-farhd` / `.kyp-farbar` / `.kyp-farscale` (the FAR bar), `.kyp-dtab` (the data
table), `.kyp-vs` (the comparison strip), `.kyp-tier` / `.kyp-tiertrack` / `.kyp-tierfill` /
`.kyp-tierscale` (quality bands).

**Additions to live families, all new selectors:** `.kyp-block .bv u` (a mono unit suffix on a display
numeral), `.kyp-subhead.sub` (sub-label), and `.kyp-recgrid.inline` (7 rules).

⚠️ **`.kyp-recgrid.inline` is scoped for a reason.** `.kyp-recgrid` ships from Step 16b as a
label-above-value grid in County Record. This section needs label-left / value-right. The modifier
means Development Potential opts in and County Record is untouched. Do not unscope it.

**Two reuse candidates I could not check:** `.kyp-hbar` (15 rules) may already do what `.kyp-farbar`
does, and `.kyp-meter` (5 rules) may cover `.kyp-tiertrack`. If either fits, use it and drop the new
family — one bar component is better than three.
