Both clear. The property truly covers at 1.90× once real expenses are counted; a DSCR-loan lender will see ~2.04× on their gross-rent formula. When they diverge, the economic number is the honest read and the loan ratio is the one on the term sheet.
Why two, not one. They answer different questions — "does the property actually cash-flow" (economic) vs "does rent cover the payment" (loan product). Showing both means a buyer isn't blindsided when a broker quotes a higher DSCR than the property's real coverage.
