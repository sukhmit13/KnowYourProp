## Cross-cutting rules (apply everywhere in this section)
1. **Line colors:** Rail (CTA L) and Metra badges/series use their REAL line colors (CTA Blue `#009fda`, Metra UP-N green `#3f8f3f`, UP-NW gold `#c8912a`; use the official color for any other line — Red/Brown/Green/Orange/Pink/Purple/Yellow). **Buses have no official color** — bus route series use the two indigo shades (`--busA`/`--busB`). Never invent a bright color for a bus (it would read as a rail line, since CTA lines cover most of the spectrum).
2. **Never use the status colors (green/red/marigold) for a chart series or line** — those mean good/serious/caution. Chart series are neutral (line colors or indigo shades).
3. **Every trend figure states its time basis in the label** — `+15% · 3-yr`, `+1% · YoY`, `-8.6% · since 2016`. The section currently mixes 36-month, YoY, and since-2016 trends with no labels; that's ambiguous. Confirm each figure's actual basis and label it.
4. **Favorability discipline:** more transit access skews positive, so ridership/access uses neutral→green (never red — a quiet stop isn't "bad"). Street traffic is the exception — see Part 3.

### Access tier (the header badge + takeaway favor) — DEFINE THIS RULE
"Strong / Moderate / Limited access" is a computed verdict, not a hand-set label. Suggested rule (pick your exact thresholds):
- **Strong** = rapid transit (L or Metra) within ~10-min walk AND 2+ frequent bus routes within ~5-min walk.
- **Moderate** = one of those two.
- **Limited** = neither.
Drive the badge class and the takeaway's favor from this. A car-dependent property must be able to come back "Limited."

## PART 3 — Street Traffic Volume (keep NEUTRAL — do not spectrum)

Do NOT apply the neutral→green ridership treatment here. High traffic is GOOD for a retail/commercial property (visibility) and BAD for a residential one (noise) — favorability flips by use case, so coloring it would mislead. Keep it a **neutral positional bar**: a plain track with a marker at the percentile, label "busier than X% of city," and neutral "Increasing/Decreasing · [basis]" pills. Keep the KPI tiles (daily vehicles, city rank, percentile) in the shared horizontal card style. This deliberate difference from the ridership bars is correct — color encodes good/bad only where there is a clear good/bad.
