## 🔴 Data-integrity rules (non-negotiable — these are why the section reads honestly)
1. **504 loans are NOT address-linked.** The SBA FOIA 504 dataset gives borrower entity, industry, amount, and CDC lender — **not** the financed property address. Never imply a 504 loan is tied to the subject or any specific building. 504 activity is **ZIP-level** context only. This must appear as a `.caution` takeaway bullet AND the 504 subsection note.
2. **7(a) records DO carry a borrower street address** + jobs supported — show them. This asymmetry (7a has address, 504 doesn't) is the point; don't flatten it.
3. **Geography is ZIP (60618), not the census tract** used by the HMDA section. Label it ZIP everywhere (subtitle, KPI subs, source). Do not silently present it as tract-level or blend the two.
4. **Activity level is NOT favorability.** High SBA volume → `.insight` (indigo), never green/`.good`. A busy corridor is directional market context, not a property-specific win. No red anywhere in this section.
