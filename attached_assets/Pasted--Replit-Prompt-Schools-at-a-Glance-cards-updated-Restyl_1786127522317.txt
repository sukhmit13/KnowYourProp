## CPS level → favorability color (fixes the red-for-Level-2 issue)
The assigned school's level dot must map to the SAME favorability tiers used site-wide — NOT red for everything below Level 1. Red is reserved for genuinely bad. Map:
- **Level 1+ / Level 1 → good** (green): `rd` green, `am` text green
- **Level 2+ → neutral** (grey `#9aa1b0`)
- **Level 2 → caution** (marigold `--caution`, text `--cautionText`)
- **Level 3 → bad** (red `--bad`, text `--badText`)
```css
.rd.lv-good{background:var(--good)} .rd.lv-neu{background:#9aa1b0} .rd.lv-cau{background:var(--caution)} .rd.lv-bad{background:var(--bad)}
.am.good .lvtxt{color:var(--good)} .am.neu .lvtxt{color:var(--ink2)} .am.cau .lvtxt{color:var(--cautionText)} .am.bad .lvtxt{color:var(--badText)}
```
Drive the class from the school's actual level. A Level 2 school is below-average, not failing — do not color it red.

## ⚠️ The verdict tier is a computed rule — and it must weigh the ASSIGNED school
"Strong / Mixed / Limited" is NOT hand-set. Compute it from the nearby schools' levels AND the assigned (boundary) school, and weigh the assigned school heavily — that's the school a family gets by default, so it's the most consequential fact.
- Do not return **Strong** when the assigned school is Level 2 or below, even if good options exist via open enrollment — that contradicts itself. In the example, High School is **Mixed** because the boundary school (Roberto Clemente) is Level 2, despite a Level-1 option nearby.
- Suggested: Strong = assigned school Level 1+/1 AND multiple strong nearby options; Mixed = decent options but assigned school is middling (Level 2+/2), or a split field; Limited = assigned Level 3 and few strong options. Pick your exact thresholds, but the assigned school must be able to pull the verdict down.
