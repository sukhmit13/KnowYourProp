## 4. Server work

### 4a. Architect — already parsed, just not passed through

`server/chicagoPermits.ts:95–104` already extracts `ownerName`, `architectName` **and**
`contractorName`. `NewConstructionSection.tsx:77` renders only the contractor. Carry `architectName`
through the `/api/new-construction` response. **No new parsing, no new request.**

### 4b. Corridor tag

`CORRIDOR_KEYWORDS` (`server/newsMonitor.ts:224`) is an exported table of **34 corridors**, each with
`names[]`, `tier`, `description`, a bounding box, and for three diagonals a `line`.

Add `corridor: { key, name, tier } | null` to each permit, DPD application, ZBA case and development
news item. The rule is **both tests, not either**:

1. the record's address string matches one of `names[]`, **and**
2. the record's point falls inside that corridor's bbox (or within ~120 m of `line` where present).

Name alone tags a permit at 7000 W Chicago Ave as the West Town Chicago Avenue corridor. Bbox alone
tags a side-street project as being on Broadway when its frontage is not. **Both, or no tag.**
Compute this server-side — the table lives there and the client never sees it.

### 4c. The unit pipeline — one address, one stage

This is the substantive work. Return three figures plus a per-record `stage`.

**Furthest stage wins.** For each normalised address across permits, DPD applications and ZBA cases:

| Stage | Condition | Counts toward |
|---|---|---|
| `permitted` | an issued new-construction permit exists | **Units under construction** when issued within 18 months |
| `proposed` | a DPD application exists **and no permit** | Potential future units |
| `zoning` | a ZBA case exists **and no permit and no DPD application** | Potential future units |
| `coverage` | a news article only | **nothing** |

`normalizeAddrForMatch` already exists (`server/routes.ts` ~`:9790`) and already does article↔permit
and article↔ZBA. Extend it to permit↔DPD and permit↔ZBA. Reuse it; do not write a second matcher.

Return:

```ts
pipeline: {
  unitsUnderConstruction: number;   // from the permit record's units field — STRUCTURED
  activePermitCount: number;        // permits behind that figure
  potentialUnits: number;           // DPD + ZBA units at non-permitted addresses — PARSED
  potentialAmbiguous: boolean;      // true if any contributing record is unitsAmbiguous
  commercialProposals: number;      // ZBA/DPD business-use cases
}
```

**Only `unitsUnderConstruction` is structured.** DPD units come from `parseUnits(description)` with
its own `unitsAmbiguous` flag (`server/dpdApplications.ts:22–23, 70`); ZBA units are regex-read from
the case `subject`; news units are regex-read from a headline.

> **Do not emit a combined total.** Adding one structured count to three parsed ones produces a
> number the product cannot defend. Render the two figures separately, exactly as the target does,
> and prefix the parsed one with `~`.

## 5. The pipeline strip

Three `.kyp-block` in a plain `<div class="kyp-blocks">` (already 3-up at top level; the single
column is inside `@media (max-width:640px)`), above subhead .1, **with no subhead of its own** —
the same way §19's commercial KPI strip opens its run.

| Block | Tone | `.bv` | `.bl` | `.bd` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ind` | `{unitsUnderConstruction}` | Units under construction | `across {activePermitCount} permits issued within 18 months · from the permit record` |
| 2 | `slate` | `~{potentialUnits}` | Potential future units | `proposed or seeking zoning relief at addresses with no permit yet · as stated in application text` |
| 3 | `slate` | `{commercialProposals}` | Commercial proposals | `special-use and map-amendment cases for business uses` |

Indigo on the first because it is the section's headline fact; slate on the others. **Not green or
red** — construction volume has no polarity.

Then one `.kyp-src pipefoot` carrying the counting rule, verbatim from the target:

> Each address is counted once, at its furthest stage: an issued permit outranks a Plan Commission
> application, which outranks a zoning appeal. News coverage is never counted. **The two figures are
> not added together** — the first is a field on the permit record, the second is read from
> application text and can be wrong where a headline figure covers more than one phase.
