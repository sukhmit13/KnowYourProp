Area Transaction Trends
Takeaway
Sales activity has cooled sharply — but that's fewer transactions, not falling prices.
Volume is down across the board. In 2025, sales fell for every residential type year-over-year — single-family −25.7%, 2–4 unit −31.2%, condo −13.2%. A broad drop in how many properties changed hands.Chart
This is transaction count, not price. A drop in the number of sales usually means higher mortgage rates and owners staying put — not declining values. Read it as a slower, less-liquid market, not a price correction. (Harder for a seller; less competition for a buyer.)
A condo-heavy ZIP with a thin commercial market. Condos are about half of all 2025 sales (682 of ~1,366). Commercial is tiny — roughly 32 sales a year — so comparable sales for a commercial property here are sparse, and its "+3.2%" is a single extra transaction, not a trend.
