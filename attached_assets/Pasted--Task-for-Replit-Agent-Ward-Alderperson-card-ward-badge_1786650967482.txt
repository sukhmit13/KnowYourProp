# Task for Replit Agent — Ward & Alderperson card: ward badge beside the name

## Goal
Small header tweak to the **Ward & Alderperson** card. Move the ward number from a small chip *above* the alderperson's name to a **larger badge to the right of the name**, on the same row. Nothing else in the card changes. Visual target: **report-alderman-head.html**. Reuse existing card CSS/tokens.

## The change
Restructure the name area into a single **flex row**: alderperson name on the left, a `WARD {n}` badge on the right, vertically centered, with `justify-content: space-between`.

- **Remove** the small "WARD 1" chip that currently sits above the name.
- **Name** stays the display/serif treatment (large). Optional: a small muted subline under it ("Alderperson · since {year}") to balance the row — keep or drop per preference.
- **Ward badge** = a **stacked tile** in the indigo-soft style:
  - a mono, uppercase, letter-spaced **"WARD"** label on top, and
  - a **large display number** below it (the ward number — this is the prominent element).
  - indigo-soft background, subtle indigo border, rounded (~12px), centered contents.
