✓Cook County Class 7c (CURE)Potentially Eligible▴
Class 7c provides a 5-year reduced assessment (16% of market value) for commercial rehabilitation or re-occupancy. Easier to qualify than Class 7a / 7b. Requires a Cook County Assessor application.
Who Can Apply
  • Commercial property owners & developers
  • Businesses re-occupying vacant commercial space
Eligibility Requirements
  • Commercial use in a qualifying area
  • Rehabilitation or re-occupancy of the property
  • Cook County Assessor application before work
Requires application / approval — not automatic. Geographic match ≠ guaranteed award.
