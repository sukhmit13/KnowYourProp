Why not a red-to-green gradient? FAR utilization has no universal "good" end — under-built means development upside, fully-built means the envelope is maximized (more income now). So the bar shows what's built (indigo) and how much headroom remains (green = the buildable opportunity). More green = more upside. The favorability lives in the green headroom and the verdict, not in a position gradient.
