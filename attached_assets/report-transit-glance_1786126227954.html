Transit Access & Ridership Strong access
The Takeaway
Strong transit access — frequent buses at the door, with the Blue Line a short walk.
Five bus corridors within a few minutes' walk. The two closest — 50 Damen and 73 Armitage — are ~3-min walks covering both north–south and east–west, and both rank in the top quarter of CTA routes by ridership.
The Western Blue Line station is a ~9-min walk (0.45 mi) — solid rapid-transit access to the Loop and O'Hare, though not immediately adjacent.
Metra (Clybourn, UP-N / UP-NW) adds a regional option ~13 min away (0.66 mi) for downtown and suburban trips.
