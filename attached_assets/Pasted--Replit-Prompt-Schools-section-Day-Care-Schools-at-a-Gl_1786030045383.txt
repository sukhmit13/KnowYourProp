### ⚠️ Two decisions to resolve before this ships (do not hardcode):
1. **The per-level rating (Strong / Mixed / Limited) needs a defined rule**, not a hand-set label. Compute it from the nearby schools' CPS levels — e.g. *Strong* = majority Level 1+/1, *Mixed* = split, *Limited* = majority Level 2/3. Pick the exact thresholds and drive the badge class from that computed value. If a rule isn't defined yet, render the badge only when a rating exists; don't invent one.
2. **Middle vs K–8 double-count.** In Chicago most elementary schools are K–8, so a separate "Middle" card often repeats the same K–8 school as Elementary. Prefer **Elementary (K–8)** and **High School** as two cards unless your data has genuinely distinct standalone middle schools. Only render a level card if that level has its own data.
