---
name: Historic status legal framing
description: Evidence-safe rules for presenting Chicago Historic Resources Survey ratings, designation, permit holds, and tax-credit eligibility.
---

Treat a CHRS survey colour as a catalogue rating, not as municipal landmark designation or National Register listing. Red and Orange ratings automatically trigger the 90-day demolition-permit hold under Municipal Code §14A-4-407.6; Yellow and Green do not. A survey rating alone does not impose general exterior-alteration restrictions or unlock Class L, Federal Historic 20%, or Illinois Historic 25% credits. State each separate eligibility requirement beside any incentive.

**Why:** Conflating survey significance, binding designation, and incentive eligibility materially overstates both development constraints and available economics.

**How to apply:** In report summaries, development sections, and print/PDF output, label every survey colour in text, distinguish rating from designation, show the 90-day hold only for Red/Orange, and present credit requirements as unmet unless independently verified.
