---
name: Professional Record evidence
description: Product scope and evidence limits for the property-scoped professional directory.
---

Professional Record is a buyer-diligence directory of who appears in the property's public records, not a recommendation or a ranking. All blocks and chips must remain neutral indigo/slate. Show the deciding body's matter outcomes, never a named person's success rate or inferred performance.

**Why:** The user explicitly prohibited verdict colors and personal performance statistics; property-level samples are small, outcomes belong to applications, and readers' intended uses differ.

**How to apply:** Preserve these limits when extending contractor, design-professional, expediter, representative, tax-attorney, or lender rows. Keep assessment dollars in Property Taxes and loan terms/reconciliation in Ownership & Title. Tax-appeal outcomes are attributed to the Board of Review, not the Assessor.

Citywide counts are deliberately excluded until Discovery's contact-slot coverage and date window can support them. Missing or ambiguous index membership means plain text, not an inactivity claim.

**Why:** The user identified incomplete permit-contact coverage and a limited rankings horizon as reasons active firms can be absent. The citywide tax-attorney aggregate also lacks a reliable outcome denominator and cannot support personal success rates.

**How to apply:** Do not add citywide counts or rates opportunistically. Use property-PIN appeal evidence for this section's matter counts, omit empty professions, and keep the fixed relative profession order with contiguous numbers derived from the report's current section position.
