---
name: CPS school assignment evidence
description: Evidence boundary between a school's attendance-area flag and the school actually assigned to an address.
---

Do not identify an address's assigned CPS school from the nearest school that has an attendance boundary, nor infer that no school is assigned when none appears in the nearby results.

**Why:** Nearby-school searches cover a limited radius and may omit the actual neighborhood school; a flag that a school has a boundary says nothing about whether the subject address lies within it. CPS says every Chicago address has a neighborhood high school with a guaranteed seat. An empty nearby boundary-school list must not be described as all-lottery or no assigned high school.

**How to apply:** Show nearby counts, distances, ratings, and per-school Boundary/Open Enrollment flags as such. Only label a school as assigned after checking the subject coordinates against current CPS attendance-boundary polygons, with school-year provenance.
