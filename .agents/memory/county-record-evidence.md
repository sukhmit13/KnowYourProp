---
name: County record evidence
description: Evidence boundaries for Cook County parcel facts, unit layouts, and commercial assessor valuation inputs.
---

Treat observed parcel and structure fields as the county record, but isolate commercial NOI, cap rate, vacancy, income, and expense figures as assessor valuation-model inputs rather than property operating results. Never display the assessor's derived investment rating as a property-quality score.

**Why:** Presenting model assumptions beside observed building facts at equal weight makes them easy to misread as actual performance or market evidence.

**How to apply:** Keep modelled figures in a visibly distinct panel with explicit epistemic labels. For residential units, never infer a split without a recorded unit count, never show fractional bedrooms, and show alternative whole-unit layouts when multiple distributions fit the same totals. Prefer a recorded commercial unit mix whenever available.
